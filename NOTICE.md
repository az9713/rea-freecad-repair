# Attribution and upstream boundary

TopoIntent's Python workbench, matching engine, tests and website are original
MIT-licensed work. FreeCAD is a separate LGPL application; it is not bundled in
the workbench ZIP. Downloaded portable runtimes and dependencies stay untracked.

`investigation/public-source/CommandDoc.cpp` and `TopoShapePyImp.cpp` are unmodified
FreeCAD sources from tag **1.1.4**, commit
`4fd3bf320d9566a27e60069fc8387448aaa3a094`. Their copyright and LGPL notices remain
in each file. The upstream `LICENSE` is included alongside them. Those files
retain their upstream license and are investigation evidence, not MIT relicensing.
`manifest.json` records upstream URLs and SHA-256 digests.

No Nxt or V2 Topological Naming source is incorporated. Names and links identify
comparison projects, not endorsements. FreeCAD/OpenCASCADE validity checks are
not a proof of a designer's complete intended semantics.
